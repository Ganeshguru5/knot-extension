# Privacy Policy — iamcandidate: Job & Referral Capture

_Last updated: October 21, 2026_

iamcandidate: Job & Referral Capture ("the extension") saves job postings and referral contacts to your own iamcandidate account. This policy explains what the extension handles and where that data goes.

## What the extension reads

Only when you open the extension (by clicking its icon or pressing Alt+K), it reads the page in the current tab to fill in its form:

- On job pages: job title, company, location, work mode, salary, job ID, job URL and job description.
- On LinkedIn profile pages: the person's name, headline, location, current company and profile URL.

The extension does not run in the background, does not read pages you don't open it on, and does not track your browsing history.

## What is sent, and where

Nothing is sent until you click **Save application** or **Add referral**. Then the details shown in the form, including any changes you made, are sent to the iamcandidate server (`ps4y8f4z9f.execute-api.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com`) and stored in your iamcandidate account. To sign in, your email and password are sent to the same server.

The extension does not send data to any other server, analytics service or advertiser.

## What is stored in your browser

- Your sign-in session token, which stays valid until you sign out (or after a year without use), and your name and email for display.
- The server address settings.
- An unsaved form draft, so input isn't lost if the popup closes. It is cleared when you save or close the browser.

Signing out removes the session from the browser.

## What we don't do

- We don't sell or transfer your data to third parties.
- We don't use or transfer your data for purposes unrelated to the extension's single purpose of saving jobs and referrals to your iamcandidate account.
- We don't use or transfer your data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

## Deleting your data

You can delete saved applications, contacts and referrals at any time in the iamcandidate app. To delete your account and all of its data, contact the developer.
